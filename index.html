How deliveries are dated and counted
Delivery date
Neither sheet records the day a creative was delivered yet, so a delivered creative is dated by its Design ETA (or its request date when the ETA is blank). Add a column named "Delivered On" to either sheet and the dashboard uses it automatically.
Creatives
Each row counts as one creative. Add a column named "No. of Creatives" to either sheet and the dashboard counts that number instead.
Delivered
Design status DONE or Delivered.
Working days
Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are not counted, so a month has 20 to 23 working days, usually 21 or 22. Public holidays are not taken out.
Per designer, per working day
Creatives completed ÷ the people who delivered at least one in the period ÷ the working days in the period (up to today). Multiply by about 21.7 for a typical month.
Comparisons
A period that is still running is compared with the same number of working days of the period before: 7 working days into November is compared with the first 7 working days of October.
Internal and External
Everything from the Paid (client) sheet is External; everything from the Marketing team sheet is Internal. The sheets keep their own names.
History
Internal (marketing) work before 2026 comes from the "2025" tab of the Marketing sheet.

Checks

Rows that make tracking less reliable. Fix them in the sheet and they drop off on the next refresh. Click a check to list its rows.

How design statuses are read
To be planned
Design status TBP (or blank) and the brief approved (content DONE or blank): ready to be picked up. Open work.
WIP
Design status WIP: in progress. Open work.
Changes requested
Design status CHANGES: shared, revisions requested. Open work.
Under review
Design status UNDER REVIEW: the team finished it; the Creative Head has not reviewed it yet. Not workload, never overdue.
Shared
Design status SHARED: with the client or another team for approval. Shared work, never overdue.
No update
Design status NO UPDATE: shared, no reply despite follow-ups. Counted as shared work, never overdue.
Delivered
Design status DONE: delivered and approved. Completed work.
Rejected
Design status REJECTED: delivered, rejected by the client. Completed work for bandwidth, not an approved delivery; a redo is a new task.
Awaiting brief
Design not started (TBP or blank) and the brief not approved: content PENDING (discussion), INCOMPLETE BRIEF, TBP (ready for final scripting), WIP (brief in progress), SHARED (brief with client) or CHANGES. Not design workload, never overdue; the Content Owner's work.
Hold
Design status HOLD, or design not started and content HOLD.
Deprioritised
Design status DEPRIORITIZED: no longer required.
Any other status
Shown as typed in the sheet, counted as open work, and listed here under "Unrecognised status".
Overdue
Open work (To be planned, WIP, Changes requested) whose Design ETA is before today.
Completed
Delivered + Rejected, dated by Delivered On, else the Design ETA. Shared and No update are not completed.
Creative_ID
SRAP = External (Paid) sheet, SRA = Internal (Marketing) sheet. IDs that do not match their sheet, are missing or repeated are listed above.
Unassigned
Tasks with no designer/editor. Counted only under Unassigned, never for a person.
Designers
Read from "Designer/Editor" (External, the Paid client sheet) and "Creator" (Internal, the Marketing sheet). Names typed together in one cell are split into each person.

The Creative Head and Admins can see when you are online, which page you are on, what you are doing there (for example a filter or the task you are editing) and your recent actions on the dashboard: pages opened, buttons clicked, boxes ticked, filters chosen, searches and updates saved. What you type in notes is never shared. It is kept for 30 minutes, then deleted.
