How deliveries are dated and counted
Delivery date
Neither sheet records the day a creative was delivered yet, so a delivered creative is dated by its Design ETA (or its request date when the ETA is blank). Add a column named "Delivered On" to either sheet and the dashboard uses it automatically.
Creatives
Each row counts as one creative. Add a column named "No. of Creatives" to either sheet and the dashboard counts that number instead.
Delivered
Design status DONE or Delivered.
Per designer, per month
Creatives delivered divided by the people who delivered at least one in the period, then by the number of months in it.
History
Marketing work before 2026 comes from the "2025" tab of the Marketing sheet.

Checks

Rows that make tracking less reliable. Fix them in the sheet and they drop off on the next refresh. Click a check to list its rows.

How design statuses are read
To be planned (TBP)
Design status TBP or blank, with the brief ready. Counts as open work.
In progress (WIP)
Design status WIP or In progress. Counts as open work.
Changes requested
Design status Changes. Counts as open work.
Under review
Design status Under review, Review or Shared: with the requester or client.
Delivered
Design status DONE or Delivered.
Awaiting brief
Design not started and content status is Incomplete brief, Pending, WIP, TBD or TBP.
On hold
Design status HOLD, On hold or No update, or content on HOLD.
Dropped
Design status Deprioritized, Rejected or Cancelled.
Any other status
Shown as typed in the sheet, counted as open work, and listed here under "Unrecognised status".
Overdue
Open work whose Design ETA is before today.
Designers
Read from "Designer/Editor" (Paid) and "Creator" (Marketing). Names typed together in one cell are split into each person.
